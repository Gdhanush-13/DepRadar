Two complementary scores

Freshness Score is the average remaining dependency health score, from 0 to 100; higher is better. Risk Score is the higher of the average pressure score and the single highest dependency pressure score. This keeps a dangerous outlier visible instead of diluting it across dozens of healthy packages; lower is better. Unresolved metadata is excluded and shown explicitly.
